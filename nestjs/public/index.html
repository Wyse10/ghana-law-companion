About Ghana Law Companion

Ghana Law Companion is an advanced, source-grounded legal assistant specialized in the 1992 Constitution of the Republic of Ghana.

Key Technical Guardrails

  • Zero Hallucination Citations: Every citation returned by the model is programmatically verified against retrieved constitutional articles. Unsupported citations are rejected at the service boundary.
  • Clause-Level Precision: Ingestion splits articles by numbered clauses while preserving lettered sub-paragraphs and context headers.
  • Hybrid Retrieval: Combines local 768-dimensional FastEmbed vector search (BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5) with lexical term coverage and constitutional reference bonuses.
  • Architectural Separation: Edge proxy (Caddy) $\rightarrow$ Backend-For-Frontend (NestJS) $\rightarrow$ Core Engine (FastAPI) $\rightarrow$ Local / PostgreSQL pgvector store.
